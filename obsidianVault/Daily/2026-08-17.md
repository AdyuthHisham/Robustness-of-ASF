# 2026-08-17

## ASF corruption sweep — full 10-sequence run, gallery rendering

Continued [[asf-corruption-study-alvis]]: fixed the `KRADAR_EVAL_GPU_IOU` segfault
inherited from the smoke-study template, added a `loss_complete_zero` effect distinct from
the existing `loss_complete`, ran an original-vs-corrupted pilot on sequences 46 and 1, then
generalized everything into a full sweep — 10 randomly-selected sequences × 10 frames ×
29 corrupted conditions (low/medium/high severity across 9 effects + 2 unconditional) + 1
original baseline each = 300 SLURM jobs. All finished; the only failures (40/300) were the
expected sparse-conv-backbone aborts on near-empty radar/LiDAR input at higher
`frame_deletion` severities.

Built a 20-frame random-sample gallery across the sweep (`build_random_sample_gallery.py`,
new script) to spot-check results without rendering all 300 frames × 29 conditions. Ran into
and fixed three real infrastructure bugs along the way: a stale duplicate SSH process racing
on shared render output, fragile multi-argument command passing through `sbatch --export`
across nested shell layers, and an accidentally-adequate-but-not-actually-correct
"does this directory exist" check for locating sensor-dump backups (fixed to verify the
specific needed files, not just the directory). Also fixed an O(n) point-cloud rendering
bottleneck at the call site rather than touching the shared `render_bev` used elsewhere.

Full detail, severity-parameter table, and the "lessons learned" list in
[[asf-corruption-study-alvis]].
