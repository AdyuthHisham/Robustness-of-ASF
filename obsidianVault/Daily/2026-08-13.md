# 2026-08-13

## K-Radar Object Class Distribution — Verified

Computed and verified the full-dataset `obj_class` histogram from `info_label_rev2/*.txt`
across all 58 sequences (34,994 label frames, 115,816 annotations). Full writeup:
[[kradar-object-class-distribution]].

Sedan (89,340) and Bus or Truck (18,401) dominate at ~93% combined, matching why
`configs/cfg_RTNH_wide.yml` only trains RTNH on those two classes. Pedestrian (6,008),
Bicycle (1,202), Motorcycle (628), and the rare cluster labels Pedestrian Group (231) /
Bicycle Group (6) are annotated but too sparse for stable detector training. `Label`
(a config placeholder class) has zero real instances.

Verified by cross-checking per-class counts sum to total object-line count, and that
`tracking_id`s are unique per frame regardless of `sensor_identify` (R/L/L1) tag — ruling
out per-sensor duplicate counting.
