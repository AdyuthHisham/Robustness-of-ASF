# 2026-08-12

## ASF Clean-vs-Noisy Corruption Study on Alvis — Complete

Ran ASF (A2Fusion) inference under 9 single-effect corruption conditions plus 2 all-zero
substitutes, on a fixed 6-frame K-Radar sample, on Alvis. Full writeup:
[[asf-corruption-study-alvis]].

### Outcome
LiDAR dominates sensitivity (gaussian noise costs 12/19 detections), radar is
mid-sensitivity, camera is nearly irrelevant (fully dead camera costs 1/19 detections).
Radar/LiDAR total blackout crashes the model outright (spconv zero-voxel assert) rather
than degrading gracefully.

### Bugs found and fixed (real, not cosmetic)
- `frame_deletion` was silently a no-op for LiDAR/camera — wrong meta-key lookup, caught
  only via per-frame sensor dumps, invisible to the existing 120 tests.
- Noise-injection seeding was not reproducible across process runs — builtin `hash()` on
  strings is salted per-process in CPython, so `seed: 42` didn't mean what it said. Fixed
  with blake2b; added a subprocess test that varies `PYTHONHASHSEED`.
- Test suite grew from 120 → 140 tests, now also runs under the container's Python 3.8.

### Gallery
Built an HTML gallery (`outputs/alvis_smoke/gallery/index.html`) with clean-vs-corrupted
BEV/camera images and box overlays. Initially skipped camera box overlays believing the
pipeline had no real projection available — wrong, found and used the pipeline's own
`T_params_seq` calibration instead. Verified the whole gallery quantitatively (density
enclosure test, polygon self-intersection check, edge-gradient test on camera) rather than
trusting that "it rendered without error" meant it was correct.
