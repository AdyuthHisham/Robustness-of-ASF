## Investigation: OS1-128 long-range coverage deficit

Completed full read-only diagnosis of why `lidar128_visible` is False with score=0.0 on frames 00070-00072 of sequence 2.

**Root cause**: OS1-128 point cloud has only ~31-65 forward points with x>80m per 360° frame (vs OS2-64's 450-540). Max y-coverage of OS1-128 long-range channels is 18.2m — objects at y≈24 are simply outside the coverage envelope. 59% of OS1-128 valid points are within 10m of the sensor (ground returns). This is a sensor/data characteristic, not a code bug.

**Evidence**: Cross-cloud analysis confirmed frames ARE aligned (70% of OS2 points have OS1 neighbor within 0.5m). Archive calibration offset (-2.54, 0.3, 0.7) makes no difference. Sensor parameters checked and confirmed correct and distinct. Ring-by-ring analysis showed only ~15 of 128 channels (rings 50-65) contribute to long-range forward detection.

Checked 10 frames total (31, 69, 70, 71, 72, 100, 200, 300, 400, 450). Pattern consistent: zero-score for distant objects (y>18, x>80), normal scores for nearby objects.
